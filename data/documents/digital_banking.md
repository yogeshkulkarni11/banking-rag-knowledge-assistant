# Digital Banking

## Internet Banking
Internet banking provides browser-based access to balances, statements, transfers, bill payments, and selected service requests. Customers should use the official bank website and avoid links received from unknown senders.

## Mobile Banking
Mobile banking provides account and payment services through an authenticated mobile application. Customers should keep the application and device operating system updated.

## Password Reset
A customer who forgets an online banking password should use the bank's official password-reset process. Identity verification may be required. Customers should not share passwords with support staff.

## Transaction Alerts
Banks may provide SMS, email, or application notifications for selected transactions. Alerts help customers identify unusual activity quickly.
