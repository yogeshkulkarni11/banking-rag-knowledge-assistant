# Cards and Payments

## Debit Card
A debit card allows customers to make purchases or cash withdrawals using funds available in the linked bank account. Customers should protect the card, PIN, and one-time passwords.

## Credit Card
A credit card provides a revolving credit facility up to an approved limit. Customers normally receive a statement and should pay at least the required minimum by the due date to avoid applicable charges.

## Card Transaction Dispute
For an unauthorized transaction, the customer should report it through the bank's official support channel as soon as possible. The bank may request transaction details and identity verification before investigating the dispute.

## UPI Safety
Customers should never share a UPI PIN or OTP. A UPI PIN is used to authorize a payment; it is not required to receive money.
