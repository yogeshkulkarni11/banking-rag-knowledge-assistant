# Retail Loan Basics

## Personal Loan
A personal loan is an unsecured loan generally used for personal expenses. Eligibility and pricing can depend on income, credit history, existing obligations, and product policy.

## Home Loan
A home loan is secured against property and is commonly used to purchase, construct, or improve a home. Tenure and interest structure depend on the product and customer profile.

## Auto Loan
An auto loan finances an eligible vehicle purchase. The financed vehicle may serve as security under the loan agreement.

## EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is a periodic payment toward a loan. For a standard amortizing loan, an EMI contains interest and principal components, with the mix changing over the loan tenure.
