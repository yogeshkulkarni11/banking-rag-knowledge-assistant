# Bank Account Types

## Savings Account
A savings account is designed for personal deposits and routine transactions. It generally earns interest and may have minimum-balance or transaction rules depending on the product.

## Current Account
A current account is commonly used by businesses that need frequent deposits, withdrawals, and payment transactions. Interest and fee structures vary by product.

## Fixed Deposit
A fixed deposit locks a principal amount for a selected tenure at an agreed interest rate. Early withdrawal may be allowed subject to product-specific penalties.

## Recurring Deposit
A recurring deposit allows a customer to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals for a chosen tenure. Maturity value depends on deposits, tenure, and the applicable interest rate.
