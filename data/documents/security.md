# Banking Security and Fraud Awareness

## Phishing
Phishing messages attempt to trick customers into revealing credentials, card information, OTPs, or other sensitive data. Customers should verify the sender and use official bank channels.

## OTP and PIN
One-time passwords, card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords, and authentication codes should never be disclosed to another person. Bank staff should not ask customers to share these secrets through unsolicited calls or messages.

## Suspicious Activity
Customers should report suspicious transactions promptly through an official support channel. The bank may temporarily restrict services while an investigation is performed.
